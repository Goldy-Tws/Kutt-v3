## 1. Executive Summary

KUTT is positioned as a **social, peer-to-peer (P2P) betting platform**. Its central idea is simple: instead of making a wager against a traditional sportsbook/house, users can make wagers **directly against friends or other identifiable users**.

The product was created around a behavior that already exists naturally: people informally make “handshake bets” with friends, in group chats, at bars, online communities, or among fans. KUTT's role is to formalize those informal bets so they can be created, accepted, tracked, settled, and experienced socially inside one product.

KUTT is therefore better understood as a combination of:

- Peer-to-peer payments
- Betting / wagering
- Prediction-style markets based on verifiable outcomes
- Social networking/community features

KUTT explicitly positions itself as **“The Social Betting Platform”** rather than simply another sportsbook.

The strongest product story is not “another place to bet on sports.” It is:

> **Turn the bets you already make with people into an actual, social, trackable experience.**

The product's differentiation comes from the combination of **known counterparties + customizable wagers + social interaction + sports and non-sports outcomes**.

## 2. What KUTT Actually Does

### Simple definition

KUTT lets users create and participate in real-money wagers with other people on events that have a verifiable outcome.

### In plain language

A user can essentially say:

> “I think Team A will beat Team B. Bet me $50.”

Instead of placing that bet against an anonymous sportsbook, KUTT can facilitate the wager between the user and another person who accepts the terms.

The key product distinction is that **a wager does not become live until a counterparty accepts the terms**.

This makes the other participant part of the experience rather than hiding them behind a sportsbook interface.

## 3. Origin Story / Why KUTT Exists

KUTT's origin story is highly useful for marketing because it is easy to understand and human.

Founder/CEO **Sim Harmon** has described making bets with friends since high school. Examples included casual sports arguments and informal “I bet you…” conversations. Historically, these bets could happen through group chats, conversations, Venmo, or verbal agreements.

The problem was not necessarily finding something to bet on. The problem was that the informal bet had no proper product layer:

- terms could be unclear;
- one person could forget to pay;
- there was no centralized tracking;
- there was no persistent record of the rivalry;
- there was no social layer around the wager.

KUTT was built to **capture, track, and monetize these “handshake bets.”**

### Marketing takeaway

The origin story creates a very natural brand message:

**“You already make bets with your friends. KUTT makes them real, trackable, and social.”**

Potential creative phrase:

> **I bet you…**

That phrase can become a strong landing-page narrative because it begins with a universally familiar social behavior rather than gambling terminology.

1. **Direct competition** — users can compete against friends or other users.
2. **Social interaction** — users can see who they are competing against and interact with them.

## 5. What Makes KUTT Different from a Traditional Sportsbook

### Traditional sportsbook model

The conventional sportsbook experience can be thought of as:

```text
USER
  ↓
SPORTSBOOK / HOUSE
  ↓
BET OUTCOME
```

The sportsbook acts as the central counterparty.

### KUTT model

KUTT emphasizes:

```text
USER A
   ↕
KUTT PLATFORM
   ↕
USER B
```

The core relationship is **person-to-person**.

KUTT facilitates the interaction rather than presenting itself primarily as the opponent.

### Experience difference

| Traditional sportsbook | KUTT positioning |
|---|---|
| Bet against a sportsbook/house | Bet against another person |
| Counterparty often invisible | Counterparty can be identifiable |
| Primarily transactional | Transaction + social experience |
| Standardized market formats | Customizable contests/wagers |
| Sports-centric | Sports + non-sports possibilities |
| Betting is the main interaction | Betting + profiles + chats + groups + leaderboards |
| Platform-centric relationship | Community-centric relationship |

### Important nuance

KUTT should **not** be described as “not betting.” It is still a real-money betting/wagering product. The differentiator is **how the wager is structured and who users compete against**.

## 10. The KUTT User Journey

The following is a conceptual product journey based on public descriptions. Current production UI should be verified before using this as exact UX documentation.

### Step 1 — Discover / Join

User discovers KUTT through:

- Friend invitation
- Sports communities
- Social media
- Partners/media personalities
- Organic search
- College communities
- Existing bettors

### Step 2 — Create account

User signs up and enters the platform.

Depending on location and current product configuration, onboarding can involve:

- Identity verification
- Geolocation
- KYC/AML-related checks
- Legal eligibility checks
- Interest/profile setup

### Step 3 — Find something to bet on

The user can browse relevant events/categories or create/find custom opportunities.

### Step 4 — Create or accept a wager

The user specifies the bet's terms and potential payout, then finds/targets an opponent or accepts an existing wager.

### Step 5 — Counterparty accepts

The wager is **not live until the other side accepts the terms**.

This is a key KUTT-specific interaction.

### Step 6 — Bet becomes active

Once accepted, both users have a live wager.

### Step 7 — Social interaction

Participants can interact through social functionality, depending on the current product implementation:

- Chat
- Profiles
- Groups
- Leaderboards
- Activity feed

### Step 8 — Outcome is determined

The agreed outcome is verified.

### Step 9 — Settlement

The winning side receives the applicable payout according to KUTT's rules/terms.

### Step 10 — Continue the relationship

After the bet, users can:

- Challenge the same person again
- Interact socially
- Compete on leaderboards
- Join groups
- Discover other users

This creates a repeat-engagement loop.

## 19. Responsible/Legal Messaging

KUTT operates in a regulated and legally sensitive industry. Therefore, landing-page design must avoid presenting broad legal claims unless the current jurisdictional facts are verified.

Avoid unverified statements such as:

- “Legal everywhere”
- “Risk-free betting”
- “Guaranteed money”
- “Guaranteed profits”
- “No risk”
- “Always win”

Prefer factual product language such as:

- “Availability varies by location.”
- “Eligibility and location restrictions apply.”
- “See our current terms and legal information.”
- “Play responsibly.”

The exact compliance copy should come from KUTT's current legal/compliance team.

## 37. Final Design Direction Recommendation

For the first round of landing-page concepts, the best strategy is to **avoid three random layouts**.

Create three genuinely different stories:

### 01 — PEOPLE OVER HOUSE

**“Bet With People. Not the House.”**

Focus: P2P differentiation.

### 02 — I BET YOU

**“Turn Your Next ‘I Bet You’ Into KUTT.”**

Focus: relatable origin story.

### 03 — SOCIAL BETTING IS THE FUTURE

**“The Social Betting Platform.”**

Focus: category creation, technology, community, and trust.

The client can then choose not only a UI style, but the **strategic story they want KUTT to own**.

## Final Context Statement

**KUTT should be understood as a people-first betting product, not simply another sportsbook.**

Its core innovation is the formalization of social/handshake bets into a real-money P2P experience where users can identify and interact with counterparties, customize wager terms, compete on sports and other verifiable outcomes, and participate in communities around those bets.

The strongest website strategy is therefore to communicate:

> **PERSON → CHALLENGE → BET → SOCIALIZE → COMPETE → VERIFY → SETTLE → DO IT AGAIN.**

That is the mental model designers, copywriters, and developers should use when translating KUTT's product into a landing-page experience.
